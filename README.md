>Ah, a fascinating subject! Did you know that the term "Artificial Intelligence" was actually coined way back in **1955**? 
>
>It was proposed by a young computer scientist named John McCarthy for a summer workshop at Dartmouth College. He and his colleagues had to invent a brand-new phrase because terms like "cybernetics" or "automata" just didn't capture the fresh, multidisciplinary approach they wanted to explore. 
>
>Even wilder? At that workshop, they optimistically believed that a small group of researchers could substantially solve the problem of machine intelligence over the course of *just one summer*! Talk about missing the deadline by a few decades. 
>
>Isn't it mind-boggling that the foundation of the technology powering our world today was laid when vacuum tubes and massive room-sized computers were still cutting-edge?
